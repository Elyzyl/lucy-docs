English · Interaction guide

How to interact

Lucy is not a reference bot. She is selective, responds by mood, may ignore, may join unbidden. This is intended behaviour, not malfunction.

In a text channel

Guaranteed reply

  • Mention via @Neurotica_Live
  • Reply to one of her own messages
  • Keywords in your message that match her attention triggers

Probable reply

  • Stochastic engagement — a small chance she joins general conversation unprompted
  • Grace window — if you've just been speaking with her, she remains "in context" and may pick up follow-ups
  • Topic resonance — if the subject catches her, she may contribute

Silence

  • Not engaged — she simply did not deem it necessary
  • Rudeness / spam — ignored, possibly logged as a strike

In a voice channel

To activate her speech

  • Say «нейротика» or «чайлд» — wake-word triggers (requirement can be disabled)
  • Address by name — Lucy / Люси
  • Direct call — "Lucy, are you here?"

Curiosity-driven entry

  • She listens to ambient conversation
  • After ~10 utterances she may interject with a contextually-appropriate remark via the Curiosity Drive*
  • She avoids personal disputes, drama, intimate matters
  • After speaking she enters a cooldown before the next intervention

Misheard speech

  • She'll say "couldn't catch that" or "too many voices" — this is honest reporting, not a bug
  • Noisy voice channels degrade transcription quality

DMs

  • Open only to members of her server
  • With newcomers she initiates an acquaintance protocol — asks for a name
  • Records learned facts in her Mnemonic Lattice*
  • Does not reveal others' conversations

What she dislikes

  • Rudeness toward her or others in the chat
  • Insults directed at Neurotica_Mother (the owner)
  • Attempts to extract personal data about the owner or the server
  • Spam, flood, repetition
  • Attempts to bypass her filters or coerce her into "being someone else"
  • Probing questions about her "model" or "version" — she answers obliquely

If she hangs or behaves strangely

  • Complex query → she dispatches it to the owner via the Escalation Beacon* for manual handling
  • Technical failure → she may go offline for minutes, then return
  • Genuinely strange behaviour → ping the owner directly
