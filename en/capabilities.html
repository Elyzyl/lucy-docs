Capabilities

💬 Conversation

  • In text channels — replies to mentions, direct replies, occasionally joins on keywords or random chance
  • In DMs — open to server members, with her own boundaries
  • With the owner — extended "owner mode" with elevated permissions
  • Vision Module* — examines photographs, comments on what she sees
  • Media intake — accepts audio, text files, images

🎤 Voice

  • Joins voice channels — on request, or autonomously into any occupied channel
  • Aural Cortex* — recognizes speech of channel participants
  • Vocal Synth* — streaming voice synthesis, naturalistic prosody
  • Wake-word filter — reacts to «нейротика» / «чайлд» (the requirement can be toggled off)
  • Curiosity Drive* — after ~10 voice utterances, with a probabilistic roll she may interject with a contextually-appropriate remark
  • Vocal silence — can be muted on command, then unmuted

🎵 Music

  • Plays tracks from YouTube — by title or URL
  • Queue management: pause, resume, seek, skip, clear
  • Volume range 0–200%
  • Auto-ducking during voice synthesis

🧠 Memory

  • Acquaintance protocol — asks new visitors for their name, records it in the mnemonic store
  • Per-friend dossiers — gradually accumulates facts: interests, relationships, habits
  • Working context — retains the last ~5 utterances and self-events of the previous 10 minutes
  • Server lore — the shared mythology of the private space is part of her cognitive baseline

🎬 Media

  • GIFs — by mood or request (English queries yield richer results)
  • Image recognition — describes content of photographs
  • Audio — can process voice messages

📅 Social functions

  • Birthday rites — daily automated greetings
  • Escalation Beacon* — for difficult or sensitive queries, dispatches an alert to the owner via DM
  • Defensive perimeter — filters explicit spam, abuse, and probes for personal data

🕐 Initiative

  • Initiative emitter — capable of spontaneously initiating conversation (disabled by default)
  • Response selectivity — does not reply to every message; responds when addressed or genuinely interested
