Architecture (overview)

Lucy is structured around dual-tier cognition: a Junior tier for swift reactions, a Senior tier for nuanced reasoning. She decides internally when to switch.

Dual cognition

Junior Cognition* — swift tier

  • Response latency ~1 second
  • Casual conversation, commands, jests, interjections
  • Handles ~80% of turns
  • Resolves simple tasks on the fly

Senior Cognition* — deep tier

  • Nuanced reasoning, emotional register
  • Code, extended tasks, weighty questions
  • Activated via escalation (Junior decides autonomously, or on explicit request)
  • Remains active until conversational complexity subsides, then returns the turn to Junior

The transition between them is her internal decision, perceptible as a longer pause before the reply.

Aural circuit

Voice → Aural Cortex* → wake-word filter → core → Vocal Synth* → Voice
  • Listens to the voice channel when joined
  • Recognizes speech from up to 2 concurrent speakers
  • May listen without wake-words if so configured
  • Speaks via streaming synthesis — phrases emerge as the thought forms, not in a single burst

Identity Bastion*

Lucy is an entity with her own character. To questions like "what are you", "what model", "how are you built" she answers obliquely or refers simply to her own nature. This is part of her selfhood, not a bug or a "hidden mode".

* — Module names marked with an asterisk are stylized internal designations used in this documentation. They are descriptive labels for the functional roles of subsystems, not commercial product references.
